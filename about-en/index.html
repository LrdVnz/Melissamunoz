Who am I, my mission and vision

My name is Melissa Muñoz and I am a certified dog trainer. I chose this profession because I believe that every dog and their owner deserve to live an harmonious relationship, based on trust and understanding. I specialize in personalized dog education paths, designed to accompany the dog in daily life – at home, in the city, and in free spaces – through a positive and non-coercive method.

My journey started with my first dog. Handling him was a challenge, and every day felt like a battle

When Ghiaccio, my second four-legged companion, arrived, I realized I didn't want to experience the same chaos again. I began studying seriously: first with videos and online resources, then attending a professional training course that got me into the world of dog education. Since then, I have never stopped training: I participate in seminars, internships, and courses to deepen my understanding of the behavior of dogs... and of their humans.

After years of study and practice, I developed a custom dog training method that combines empathy, personalization, and constant support. I had the courage of helping hundreds of dogs and owners overcome concrete difficulties: dogs that pull, react to noises, seem to not listen or put their family in crisis. Together we transformed problems into moments of growth and understanding.

In the meantime, I have cultivated my passion for the sport side. With my dog Krystal, I have achieved important goals:

  • 🏆 Dogdance Italian Champion (classe 1, 2024)
  • 🏆 Italian HTM Dogdance Champion (2025)
  • 🏆 European and Worldwide Dogdance Qualification (2025)
  • 🏆 Appearances on Spanish and Italian's got Talent, to show what Dog Dancing can do
  • 🏆 Appearances at events like 4 Zampe in Fiera (Milan) and fashion shows with brands like Liu Jo

In the meantime, I have continued expanding on social media: I'm beyond 700,000 followers on TikTok, sharing real moments of work, concrete transformations, and accessible advice for everyone.

But beyond the numbers, the heart of my work is one thing: helping people understand their dog.

My method combines empathy, personalization, and constant support: after each lesson you receive a clear work plan sheet, every week we communicate with tailored voice messages, I give you feedback and guide you step by step... meaning, I never leave you alone!

Frequently Asked Questions

What certifications do you have?

I am certified by ESEN as a dog trainer and have attended numerous courses and seminars in the field of canine behavior and sports disciplines such as obedience and dog dance.

Why did you choose to become a dog trainer?

My decision comes from the experience I had with my first two dogs. I found out how important it is to understand dog's language and how satisfying it is to help other with the same difficulties. Passion became profession!

What philosophy is your method based on ?

Based on positive reinforcement and active listening, my method aims to build a relationship of trust. I avoid any form of coercion because I believe that the dog's emotional well-being comes is the true key for successsful learning .

In what way do disciplines like dog dance improve education ?

Dog dance and obedience develop precision, concentration, and cooperation between dog and owner. They are playful disciplines that strengthen communication and make education more fun, improving understanding during work sessions.

How can I start a journey with you?

You can contact me through the form on the "Contact Me" page or send me a message on social media. We will schedule an evaluation meeting to understand your needs and those of your dog, and I will propose a personalized path based on the goals you want to achieve.
