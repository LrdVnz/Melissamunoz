Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a dog training journey last ?

The duration depends on the dog and the owner's goals. Generally, the course includes an initial assessment, a series of practical lessons, and ongoing support. We will define together the number of sessions needed to achieve lasting results.

My dog is an adult ! Can he still learn ?

Absolutely! Dogs can learn at any age, although they may require different times compared to puppies. With the right dog training method and a positive approach, even an adult dog can change its behaviors and develop new skills.

Where do the lessons take place ?

The lessons take place in everyday setting: your home, your city, your favorite park or other places where you puppy can learn proper behavior. This gives us instant application to real life situations.

Can I start with a puppy or dog of any race?

Yes! My method is made for both puppies and adults of all breeds and sizes. Working with puppies allows to set the right habits from the start, but even older dogs can learn to behave better. I adapt the techniques to the characteristics of each dog and the needs of the owner, ensuring maximum well-being and concrete results.
